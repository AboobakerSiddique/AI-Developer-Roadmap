🚀 **Built: Fitscope — AI Resume Analyzer**

I recently built **Fitscope**, an AI-powered resume analysis tool that evaluates how well a candidate's profile matches a specific job description.

Instead of relying on simple keyword matching, Fitscope analyzes the context of both documents and provides:

• 📄 Resume & Job Description PDF/Text input
• 🎯 Overall compatibility score
• ✅ Matched skills
• ❌ Missing skills
• 💪 Strengths & weaknesses
• 📊 Requirement-by-requirement match breakdown
• 💡 AI-generated recommendation

### Tech Stack

**Python • FastAPI • LangChain • Gemini • Pydantic • Next.js • TypeScript • Tailwind CSS**

The project helped me put **structured LLM output, Pydantic validation, document processing, and AI application development** into practice.
